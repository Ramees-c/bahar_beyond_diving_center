THE INSTRUCTOR

Meet Your Guide

HEAD INSTRUCTOR

Afshin

I am an ocean lover who turned passion into purpose. What started as a career in business development eventually gave way to something far more meaningful: the call of the sea.

I made the bold decision to follow that call and became a professional scuba diving instructor, dedicating my life to exploring and sharing the underwater world. Over the years, I have certified hundreds of divers across Southeast Asia and the Middle East, guiding people of all backgrounds into a world many have never experienced before.

For me, diving is more than just a skill. It is a way to connect, to learn, and to truly disconnect from the noise of everyday life. I am deeply passionate about marine life and coral ecosystems, and I take pride in not just teaching diving, but also inspiring awareness and respect for the ocean.

Every dive is an opportunity to witness something extraordinary, and I believe everyone deserves to experience that feeling. Whether it is a first time dive, a certification course, or a private boat trip to the stunning Daymaniyat Islands, my goal is simple: to create unforgettable moments and help people discover a deeper connection with the ocean.

Because for me, there is no place more peaceful, more powerful, and more unifying than the sea.
