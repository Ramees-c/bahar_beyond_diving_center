PACKAGE DETAILS

Daymaniyat Island Private Package

Price 240 OMR
Duration 5-6 Hours
Location Daymaniyat Islands
Group Size Up to 12

Overview

Discover the beauty of Oman's marine reserve with a private Daymaniyat Island package. This carefully crafted experience combines snorkeling, island exploration, and exclusive boat transport for a memorable day on the water. Perfect for families, couples, and small groups who want a premium, worry-free escape.

What’s Included

  • Private boat transfer
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Food, water, and refreshments
  • Island permits and guide services
  • Insurance coverage during the trip

Itinerary

  • Depart from Al Mouj Marina at 8:30 AM
  • Arrive at Daymaniyat Islands by 9:30 AM
  • Enjoy snorkeling and beach time at protected locations
  • Relax on the island with refreshments
  • Return to the marina by 2:00 PM

Requirements

  • Comfortable swimming ability
  • Light physical fitness
  • Valid travel documents for non-residents
  • Arrival at the marina 30 minutes before departure
