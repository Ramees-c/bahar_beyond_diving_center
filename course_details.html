CERTIFICATION

PADI Open Water Diver

Price 200 OMR
Level Certification
Duration 3-5 Days
Max Depth 18 Meters
Min. Age 10 Years

Overview

The PADI Open Water Diver course is the world's most popular entry-level scuba certification. It qualifies you to dive independently with a buddy up to 18 meters anywhere in the world. Whether you want to explore vibrant reefs or see majestic marine life, this is where your underwater journey begins.

What the Course Includes

  • Theory (Knowledge Development) – Learn diving principles, safety rules, pressure, equipment, and underwater communication
  • Confined Water Training – Practice basic scuba skills in a pool or shallow controlled environment
  • Open Water Dives (4 dives) – Real dives in the ocean under instructor supervision

What You'll Learn

  • Diving principles and safety rules (Theory)
  • Basic scuba skills in controlled water
  • Buoyancy control and underwater navigation
  • Equipment assembly and maintenance

Certification Benefits

  • Dive independently with a buddy (up to 18m)
  • Recognized globally at dive centers and resorts
  • Pathway to advanced diving courses like Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver, and specialties

Requirements

  • Basic swimming ability
  • Good physical health
  • Completion of a medical questionnaire
