DAY TRIP ADVENTURE

Daymaniyat Island Private Excursion

Experience Overview:

Experience the untouched beauty of the Daymaniyat Islands on an exclusive private boat trip designed for relaxation, exploration, and adventure in one of Oman's most stunning marine reserves. This 5-6 hour journey takes you across crystal-clear waters to a protected archipelago known for its vibrant coral reefs, white sandy beaches, and rich marine life. Ideal for couples, families, or small groups, the trip offers a personalized escape away from crowds, with complete flexibility in timing and activities.

240 OMR
5-6 HOURS
AL MOUJ MARINA

Itinerary:

  • Depart from Al Mouj Marina @ 8:30am
  • Arrival at Daymaniyat Island @ 9:30am
  • Snorkel / Chill time at 2-3 locations as per weather
  • Head Back to Marina @ 1:00pm
  • Return to Al Mouj Marina @ 2:00pm

What's Included:

  • Snorkel gear
  • Sandwiches & fruits
  • Juice & water
  • Life jackets
  • Island entry permits
  • Tour Guide

GOLDEN HOUR ESCAPE

Daymaniyat Islands Private Sunset Boat Tour

Experience Overview:

Experience the magical golden-hour beauty of the Daymaniyat Islands on a private sunset boat tour designed for serenity, romance, and unforgettable coastal views. This exclusive 4-5 hour journey takes you across calm evening waters as the sun sets over Oman's most pristine island reserve. Guests can enjoy swimming in crystal-clear waters, light snorkeling, relaxing on deck, and capturing stunning sunset views over turquoise horizons.

240 OMR
4-5 HOURS
AL MOUJ MARINA

Itinerary:

  • Depart from Al Mouj Marina @ 2:00pm
  • Arrival at Daymaniyat Island @ 2:45pm

  • Sunset point
  • Head Back to Marina @ 5:30pm
  • Return to Al Mouj Marina @ 6:30pm
