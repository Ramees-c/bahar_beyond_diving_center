PADI Diving Courses

BEGINNER EXPERIENCE

Discovery Scuba Diving

What it is:

A PADI Discovery Scuba Diving (DSD) experience is a beginner-friendly introduction to scuba diving designed for people who have never dived before. It's a supervised, non-certification program that lets you try diving safely in shallow, controlled conditions.

50 OMR / 2 Dives
40 OMR / 1 Dive

What you'll learn:

  • Basic scuba equipment use (mask, regulator, fins, tank)
  • Simple underwater communication signals
  • Breathing underwater and equalizing pressure
  • Safety rules and instructor-led control throughout the dive

Typical experience flow:

  • Brief theory session (20–30 minutes)
  • Practice in shallow water or pool
  • Guided open-water dive (usually max 5–12 meters depth)
  • Total experience often lasts half a day to one day

ENTRY-LEVEL CERTIFICATION

PADI Open Water Diver Course

What it is:

The PADI Open Water Diver course is the world's most popular entry-level scuba certification, offered by PADI. It qualifies you to dive independently with a buddy up to 18 meters anywhere in the world.

200 OMR
3-5 DAYS
18M DEPTH

What the course includes:

  • Theory (Knowledge Development) – Learn diving principles, safety rules, pressure, equipment, and underwater communication
  • Confined Water Training – Practice basic scuba skills in a pool or shallow controlled environment
  • Open Water Dives (4 dives) – Real dives in the ocean under instructor supervision

Requirements:

  • Minimum age: 10–15+ depending on certification level (Junior/Standard Open Water)
  • Basic swimming ability
  • Good health (medical questionnaire required)

Skills you will learn:

  • Setting up and using scuba equipment
  • Breathing and buoyancy control underwater
  • Mask clearing and regulator recovery
  • Safe ascent and descent techniques
  • Underwater navigation basics

Certification benefits:

  • Dive independently with a buddy (up to 18m)
  • Recognized globally at dive centers and resorts
  • Pathway to advanced diving courses like Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver, and specialties

ADVANCED SKILLS

PADI Advanced Open Water Course

What it is:

The PADI Advanced Open Water Diver course is the next step after Open Water, designed to build confidence, expand skills, and let you explore deeper and more specialized diving experiences.

190 OMR
2-3 DAYS
30M DEPTH

What the course includes:

You complete 5 adventure dives, each focused on a different type of diving experience:

  • Deep Dive – Learn to safely dive up to 30 meters
  • Underwater Navigation – Improve compass and natural navigation skills
  • 3 Elective Dives – Choose based on interest (e.g., night diving, drift diving, wreck diving, buoyancy control, fish identification)

Requirements:

  • Must hold PADI Open Water Diver certification (or equivalent)
  • Minimum age: usually 12–15+ depending on agency rules and dive type
  • Basic diving experience and comfort underwater

Skills you will learn:

  • Advanced buoyancy control for smoother diving
  • Deep diving safety techniques and planning
  • Better underwater navigation and awareness
  • Specialty diving basics depending on chosen dives

Certification benefits:

  • Dive deeper up to 30 meters
  • Gain experience in different dive environments
  • Unlock access to specialty courses (wreck, night, rescue, etc.)
  • Improve confidence and dive control in real conditions

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS

PADI EFR + Rescue Course

What it is:

The PADI Rescue Diver course and Emergency First Response (EFR) training by PADI are advanced-level programs designed to improve diver safety, confidence, and emergency handling skills both underwater and on land.

270 OMR
3-4 DAYS

Emergency First Response (EFR):

EFR is a first aid and CPR training program that prepares you to respond to medical emergencies.

  • Primary and secondary care (CPR, first aid, AED use)
  • Injury assessment and emergency response steps
  • Choking management and shock treatment
  • Practical, scenario-based training for real-life situations

Requirements:

  • Must be a PADI Advanced Open Water Diver (or equivalent)
  • Valid EFR certification (or complete it alongside the course)
  • Minimum age: generally 12–15+ depending on certification level
  • Good swimming ability and diving experience

PADI Rescue Diver Course:

The Rescue Diver course focuses on preventing and managing diving emergencies.

  • Self-rescue techniques and diver stress management
  • Assisting tired, panicked, or unresponsive divers
  • Underwater search and recovery basics
  • Emergency planning and rescue scenarios

Certification benefits:

  • Builds confidence in handling real diving emergencies
  • Essential step toward becoming a professional diver (Divemaster)
  • Enhances safety awareness for all types of diving
  • Recognized globally in professional diving progression
