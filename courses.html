PADI Diving Courses

BEGINNER

Discovery Scuba diving

A PADI Discovery Scuba Diving (DSD) experience is a beginner-friendly introduction to scuba diving designed for people who have never dived before. It’s a supervised, non-certification program that lets you try diving safely in shallow, controlled conditions.

20–30 MINUTES
USUALLY MAX 5–12 METERS DEPTH
50 OMR FOR 2 DIVES, 40 OMR FOR 1 DIVE
Intermediate

Padi Advance Open Water Course

The PADI Advanced Open Water Diver course by PADI is the next step after Open Water, designed to build confidence, expand skills, and let you explore deeper and more specialized diving experiences.

TYPICALLY 2 TO 3 DAYS, CAN BE COMPLETED OVER A WEEKEND IN MANY DIVE CENTERS, FLEXIBLE SCHEDULING DEPENDING ON LOCATION AND CONDITIONS
DEEPER UP TO 30 METERS
190 OMR
Certification

Padi Open water course

The PADI Open Water Diver course is the world’s most popular entry-level scuba certification, offered by PADI. It qualifies you to dive independently with a buddy up to 18 meters anywhere in the world.

TYPICALLY 3 TO 5 DAYS (CAN VARY BASED ON LOCATION AND SCHEDULE), FLEXIBLE E-LEARNING OPTION AVAILABLE FOR THEORY BEFORE ARRIVAL
UP TO 18M DEPTH
240 OMR
