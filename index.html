Discovery Scuba diving

A PADI Discovery Scuba Diving (DSD) experience is a beginner-friendly introduction to scuba diving designed for people who have never dived before. It’s a supervised, non-certification program that lets you try diving safely in shallow, controlled conditions.

Padi Open water course

The PADI Open Water Diver course is the world’s most popular entry-level scuba certification, offered by PADI. It qualifies you to dive independently with a buddy up to 18 meters anywhere in the world.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Basic swimming ability and good health are required. For the Discovery Scuba program, no prior experience is needed—we'll teach you everything you need to know for your first dive.

For all courses and island trips, we provide all necessary professional equipment, including masks, fins, tanks, and wetsuits. Just bring your swimwear and a sense of adventure!

Scuba diving is very safe when practiced under professional supervision. We strictly follow PADI safety standards, maintain a low student-to-instructor ratio, and ensure all equipment is regularly serviced.

A PADI Open Water Diver course typically takes 3 to 5 days to complete. This includes theory, shallow water skill practice, and 4 open water ocean dives.

Diving is great year-round! For the best visibility and whale shark sightings at the Daymaniyat Islands, the peak season is usually between July and October.
